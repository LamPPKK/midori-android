Xanh Browser is a privacy-minded Android browser powered by the system WebView.

Browse with independent tabs, restore your open pages after process death, and keep your history, bookmarks and download records in a local Room database. Xanh Browser blocks mixed content, disables cleartext production traffic, enables Safe Browsing and refuses unknown external link schemes.

The interface adapts to phones, tablets and foldables and supports predictive back, file uploads, geolocation prompts, sharing, desktop mode and asynchronous privacy clearing.
